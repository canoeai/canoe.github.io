Half-day sessions that bring your whole crew up to speed

AI-Powered Productivity

Most teams are using AI for one thing, at best. This workshop shows practical tools and workflows to reclaim hours every week across writing, admin, research and reporting.

  • Audit where time is quietly being lost
  • Match the right tool to the right task
  • Build simple, repeatable workflows
  • Leave with a one-page productivity plan

Responsible AI: Governance Basics

The policies and guardrails every business needs before scaling AI use, explained without the jargon.

  • Understand the real risks (and the ones that are overhyped)
  • Draft a working AI use policy
  • Set clear rules for data and confidentiality
  • Assign ownership so governance does not quietly lapse

AI for Growth: Marketing & Sales

Where AI genuinely moves the needle on pipeline and revenue, and where it is a distraction.

  • Content and campaign workflows that hold your brand voice
  • Using AI in prospecting without sounding like a robot
  • Building a simple growth experiment plan

Building an AI-Ready Team & Culture

  • Address the real fears (job security, competence, trust)
  • Build a culture of safe experimentation
  • Identify and empower your internal champions

Prompting & Tools Skills for Leaders

A practical, hands-on session for leadership teams to get genuinely capable with the tools, not just aware of them.

  • Core prompting techniques that actually work
  • Choosing the right tool for the right job
