From growth coach to AI navigator

I'm Richard Gray, and I've spent over 20 years helping ambitious founders and leadership teams get out of the day-to-day grind and back in control of where their business is heading.

I've watched plenty of trends come and go. AI is different. It is not a fad, and it is not optional, but most of the advice out there is either breathless hype or dense enough to put people off before they start. Neither helps a business owner actually decide what to do on a Monday morning.

So I did what I always do: I got trained properly, tested it in my own business first, and built a practical approach around what actually works. That is what Canoe now brings to clients, the same structured, no-nonsense coaching that has always been at the heart of this business, now aimed at helping you use AI well, safely, and profitably.

I still believe change starts with one honest conversation about where you are and where you want to be. That has not changed. Only the map has.
