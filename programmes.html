The Bearing Check

A free, no-obligation call. We work out where your business stands with AI right now, where the real opportunity sits, and whether it makes sense to go further together. Most businesses leave this call with at least one thing they can action immediately, whether or not we ever work together again.

Free  ·  Online or in person

Diagnostic

AI Readiness Chart

A structured audit of your current tools, data, team capability, risk exposure and opportunity areas. You'll receive a one-page chart, in plain English, that maps out where to focus first, what to fix before scaling, and what to leave well alone for now.

  • Half-day working session with you and key team members
  • Review of current tools, workflows and data handling
  • Your one-page AI Readiness Chart and priority roadmap

From £495  ·  One-off

Flagship programme

The 90-Day Navigator

Our core programme. Over 90 days, we move from chart to course: implementing your AI strategy, building in governance basics, and freeing up real time and margin, with fortnightly working sessions and hands-on support between them.

  • Kicks off with your AI Readiness Chart (included)
  • Fortnightly one-to-one sessions across 90 days
  • Two to three prioritised AI use cases implemented, not just discussed
  • Baseline governance and policy put in place
  • One-page 90-day plan with clear accountability checkpoints

From £2,950  ·  Payment plans available

Safe Passage

AI tools and regulation move quickly. Safe Passage is ongoing governance and advisory support so your policies, your team's practice and your risk position stay current, without you having to track it all yourself.

  • Direct access for quick advisory questions as they come up
  • Quarterly team check-in to keep practice aligned with policy
